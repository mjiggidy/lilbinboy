# Lil' Disclaimer Boy

Lil' Bin Boy is a free, open-source program designed to view Avid bins
quickly and easily on any system.

## Use at your own lil' risk

Lil' Bin Boy is designed and intended to operate as a read-only viewer
and will not modify your project data. It does save its own preferences
to your OS's standard application data directory, but that's it.

developer is not liable for lost data, corrupted media, missed deadlines,
low audience scores, or any other damages arising from use of this
application. Nonetheless, keeping backups of your projects is always
encouraged!
